# MTG sealed prerelease variant audit

Audit date: 2026-07-31

## Rule

The checklist requires one copy of each distinctly named sealed prerelease pack or
kit. Packaging tied to a guild, clan, faction, color/path, character, college, or
other named choice is a separate slot. Duplicate copies of the same variant do not
increase completion. Random contents inside otherwise identical packaging (promo,
die color, stamped card, or booster contents) do not create variants.

The generated checklist now has **69 product rows and 148 distinct required
variants**. The 49 rows not listed below are single generic sealed products and keep
one `Standard Prerelease Pack` slot.

## Evidence and decisions

- Wizards' [Magic 2015 Prerelease Primer](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/magic-2015-prerelease-primer)
  names the five Hunt choices.
- Wizards' [Dragons of Tarkir Prerelease Primer](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/dragons-of-tarkir-prerelease-primer)
  presents the five dragonlord-aligned boxes and names Dromoka, Ojutai, Silumgar,
  Kolaghan, and Atarka.
- Wizards' [The Brothers' War product article](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/whats-inside-the-brothers-war-boosters)
  identifies separate Urza- and Mishra-themed packaging; the official first-look
  images name them Urza's Iron Alliance and Mishra's Burnished Banner.
- Wizards' [Tarkir: Dragonstorm Prerelease Guide](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/tarkir-dragonstorm-prerelease-guide)
  explicitly says there are five different packs and names all five clans.
- Wizards' [Avatar Prerelease Guide](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/avatar-the-last-airbender-prerelease-guide)
  documents the five character-associated packs/boosters.
- Wizards' [Modern Horizons 2 Product Overview](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/modern-horizons-2-product-overview-2021-05-21)
  calls MH2 the expansion of full Prerelease Packs beyond Standard-focused sets.
  This supports adding MH2 while leaving Modern Horizons 1 excluded.
- Wizards documents a single Prerelease Pack for
  [Commander Legends: Battle for Baldur's Gate](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-battle-for-baldurs-gate-product-overview)
  and [Modern Horizons 3](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-modern-horizons-3).
- Wizards' [Mirrodin Besieged Prerelease Primer](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/mirrodin-besieged-prerelease-primer)
  confirms special prerelease-only, single-faction sealed packs. The independent
  [Collation Project record](https://www.lethe.xyz/mtg/collation/mbs.html) documents
  the store display as 18 Mirran and 18 Phyrexian faction packs. Because the tracked
  object is each distinct sealed pack/kit variant, this is two required variants;
  the mixed 36-pack display is not a third variant.
- Wizards' [Aetherdrift Prerelease Guide](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/the-guide-to-your-first-prerelease-with-aetherdrift)
  describes one generic pack containing a die selected from five color variations.
  A random accessory is not separately named sealed packaging, so Aetherdrift stays
  at one. The same rule was applied throughout the generic-row re-audit.

## Intentional exclusions

- Modern Horizons 1 had prerelease events but predates Wizards' non-Standard full
  Prerelease Pack rollout documented for Modern Horizons 2.
- The original Commander Legends had a prerelease event/draft experience but no
  separately sold boxed Prerelease Pack in its official product lineup; Battle for
  Baldur's Gate did and is now included.
- Other pre-Return to Ravnica releases remain excluded unless a distinct sealed
  prerelease product is verified; Mirrodin Besieged is the documented exception.
- Masters/reprint, Commander, Jumpstart/supplemental, and Collector-only releases
  remain excluded when their official product lineup has no sealed Prerelease Pack.
- Differently colored random dice, promo-card possibilities, and ordinary wrapper
  variation inside a generic kit do not create additional sealed variants.
